Privacy Policy

KW Personal Finance — Last updated: October 2026

KW Personal Finance is a private, non-commercial personal finance application used exclusively by its owner to view and analyse the owner's own financial account information.

Information processed

The application may process read-only financial information belonging to the application owner, including account information, account balances and transaction history.

The application does not provide payment initiation functionality and cannot initiate transfers, payments, standing orders or other banking transactions.

Bank authentication

Bank authentication and consent are performed through the relevant financial institution and, where applicable, Enable Banking.

KW Personal Finance does not store the owner's online banking passwords or bank authentication credentials.

Storage and use of financial information

Financial information retrieved by the application is used solely for the owner's personal financial analysis.

Where practical, application data is stored on the owner's private, self-hosted infrastructure.

Financial information is not sold, used for advertising, or provided to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-party account information services

KW Personal Finance may use Enable Banking to obtain read-only account information from supported financial institutions.

Such processing is limited to what is required to provide account information services requested and authorised by the application owner.

Data subject and access

The application is intended solely for use by its owner and is not offered as a service to other users. The application only accesses accounts for which the owner has explicitly provided authorisation.

Contact

Privacy and data-protection enquiries may be sent to the application owner using the data-protection contact address supplied to Enable Banking.
