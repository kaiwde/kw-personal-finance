Purpose

KW Personal Finance is a private, non-commercial personal finance application created exclusively for use by its owner.

It is intended to provide read-only access to the owner's own financial account information for personal financial analysis.

Read-only functionality

The application is designed as an account-information-only application.

It does not initiate payments, bank transfers, standing orders, card transactions, or modifications to financial accounts.

Authorised accounts

The application may access only financial accounts belonging to the application owner and for which the owner has explicitly provided authorisation.

Third-party services

The application may use third-party account information infrastructure, including Enable Banking, to retrieve information from supported financial institutions.

Authentication and consent with a financial institution are performed using the institution's authorised authentication process.

Personal use

KW Personal Finance is not offered commercially and is not made available as a financial service to third parties.

No financial advice

Information displayed by the application is for personal informational purposes only and does not constitute financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

Contact
